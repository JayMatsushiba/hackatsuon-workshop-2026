### ⚠ CRITICAL CAVEAT ON THE BONITO "#1" CLAIM
The official tourism site (katsuomenu2026, published ~1 July 2026) says "Japan's number one producer of fresh bonito for **28 consecutive years**." But regional broadcaster **khb (16 June 2026)** reports: **2025 was a record-poor year — only 4,372 t — and the consecutive-#1 streak (stated there as 28 years) was BROKEN.** As of 15 June 2026 the 2026 season had landed ~2,680 t (476 t on 12 June, >1,000 t on 13 June), already >60% of all of 2025, and prices were ~20% below the prior year; an expert predicted Sanriku bonito would return to normal and that Kesennuma would "definitely recapture" #1 — but **as of that article Kesennuma had NOT regained #1**. Meanwhile the en.kesennuma-kanko.jp homepage carried a line about Kesennuma "regaining top national ranking in June 2026" (my fetch summarised it that way — I did not see the underlying article, so **do not publish this**).

**Recommendation for web copy:** write "long the top port in Japan for fresh bonito" or "top for 28 consecutive years until 2025" rather than a live claim. Sources: https://en.kesennuma-kanko.jp/katsuomenu2026/ · https://www.khb-tv.co.jp/news/16649032 · https://www.khb-tv.co.jp/news/16318341

Also note: **2025 was the 350th anniversary of pole-and-line bonito fishing arriving in Kesennuma** — from a search snippet only, `[U]`, verify.

## 2. NAMED PLACES TO EAT & DRINK

### 2A. Kesennuma "Umi no Ichi" / Sea Market — 気仙沼「海の市」
- **English:** Kesennuma Fish Market building / "Umi no Ichi" (Sea Market) · **JP:** 気仙沼 海の市
- **What:** The flagship gourmet-and-souvenir complex **adjacent to the working city fish market**; ground floor = fish shops, processed-seafood shops, restaurants; 2nd floor = **Tourist Service Center**, **Shark Museum (シャークミュージアム — billed as Japan's only shark museum)** and **Ice Aquarium (氷の水族館)**. Reopened 2014 after the tsunami.
- **Area:** 2-13 Minatomachi / 魚市場前, inner-bay port area. ~7 min by car from Kesennuma Port IC. Parking 112 standard + 8 coach bays. Nearest BRT stop **Minami-Kesennuma**; city loop bus stop **"Umi no Ichi Mae."**
- **Hours `[VERIFY]`:** Oct–Apr **8:00–17:00**; May–Sep **8:00–17:30** (the English tourism page said "8:00–18:00 daily, year-round, no closures" — **the two official pages conflict; trust uminoichi.com**). Closed days: **irregular (不定休) Jan–June; no closures July–Dec** — there is a published calendar. Shark Museum: May–Sep 9:00–17:30 (last entry 16:30), Oct–Apr 9:00–17:00 (last entry 16:00). Ice Aquarium: May–Sep 10:00–17:30, Oct–Apr 10:00–17:00, **from 9:00 on weekends/holidays**. TEL 0226-24-5755.
- **Food tenants `[C-tr]`:** **いちば寿司 Ichiba Sushi** (conveyor-belt sushi, 2F, uses fukahire/katsuo/sanma; 10:00–20:00, LO 19:30, weekdays to 19:00) · **リアスキッチン Rias Kitchen** (kaisen-don + fukahire dishes; 10:00–14:30 and 17:00–20:00) · **まぐろ屋 Maguro-ya** (cheap tuna donburi; 11:00–14:00, 17:30–21:00).
- **Shopping tenants `[C-tr]`:** 濱喜 Hamaki (fresh fish at wholesale prices, **closed Wed**) · 阿部長 Abe-chō (fresh seafood, live scallop/abalone) · MermaidStyle (shark-fin soup, Sanriku seaweed, souvenirs) · うつみ Utsumi (sweets/bread with shark fin, yuzu) · かに物語 Kani Monogatari (deep-sea red crab, **closed Wed**) · 横田屋本店 Yokotaya Honten (nori, salted uni, dried seafood) · 磯蔵 Isokura (roe-bearing squid, seaweed) · 伊八郎 Ihachirō (tsukudani, dried goods) · 海の子ホヤぼーや character shop.

### 2B. Kesennuma City Fish Market — 気仙沼市魚市場
- **What:** The working municipal wholesale market; **you cannot enter the floor for hygiene reasons**, but there is a **long 2nd-floor observation deck running the full length of the market**, plus a **"Fisheries Information Facility" (水産情報等発信施設)** with a 3-screen theatre showing tuna longlining, a forklift simulator, and a virtual market experience. A **cooking studio** with bay views is used for classes/community events. Rooftop parking gives bay panoramas.
- **Hours / viewing `[VERIFY]`:** official site says **6:30–17:00**; the recommended-route page gives viewing **7:00–12:00** with **6:00–8:00 the best window** for landings and bidding. Closed: **year-end/New Year** on the official site; **japan-guide says "closed Sundays and select days."** The market publishes a landings/closure feed (水揚情報 入船・休場) and a closed-day PDF calendar. **Admission appears free** (japan-guide says free; official page states no fee). TEL 0226-22-7119 (weekdays only).
- **What you see by season `[C-tr]`:** flounder, sea bream, salmon, cod year-round; **bonito May–October**; swordfish landings and auctions on the 1F/2F viewing areas; **tuna landed year-round**; the deck sections split small fish vs large fish incl. **tuna and sharks**.
- **English:** site is essentially Japanese-only.
- Sources: https://kesennuma-uoichiba.jp/ · https://kesennuma-uoichiba.jp/tourroute/ · https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5036.html (**last updated 20 Feb 2025**)

### 2C. Naiwan / ないわん — the inner-bay food-hall cluster (the single best walkable eating zone)

## 4. PRACTICAL NOTES FOR A WEEKEND OF EATING

### Walkability `[C]` / `[C-tr]`
- **The one genuinely walkable eating zone is the inner-bay port strip**: **Naiwan (4 buildings) ↔ Kakuboshi & Otokoyama brewery shops ↔ Umi no Ichi ↔ Mishione Yokochō ↔ the fish market observation deck**, plus the Murasaki-jinja-mae nightlife street in Minamichō. VISIT KESENNUMA states all three izakaya clusters "should be in walking distance of your accommodation" if you stay near the port.
- **JR East's model course** (updated 20 Nov 2024) is a **~3.5 hour walking loop from Kesennuma Station** taking in the registered-cultural-property brewery shops, Naiwan, and Umi no Ichi. It was used for a JR "Station Hiking" programme with a leaflet from the **Kesennuma Station tourist information office**.
- **Exact walking minutes between these points are NOT confirmed anywhere I fetched.** The only walking figure I have: **VISIT MIYAGI says Kesennuma Plaza Hotel is a 15-minute walk to the market.** `[C]` Do not invent the rest.
- **Not walkable — car or bus required:** Oshima island (VISIT MIYAGI gives a **29-minute bus ride**; also reachable via the Oshima Ōhashi bridge and by Oshima Kisen boat from the Umareru terminal), Karakuwa peninsula (**Banya oyster hut is "car access only"**), Michi-no-Eki Ōya-Kaigan / UMICOCO (in Motoyoshi-chō, 〒988-0273 本吉町三島9), Anchor Coffee Mother Port (Tateyama), and Yakiniku Kuriko / hormone shops (scattered; Ofuku is in Matsuzaki).
- **Local transport:** city **loop bus (循環バス)** with an "Umi no Ichi Mae" stop; **Miyakō Bus** (Kesennuma office 0226-22-7163); **JR Kesennuma Line BRT** — nearest stop to Umi no Ichi is **Minami-Kesennuma**. Taxis exist. **Rental bicycles are available at the Kesennuma Station-front tourist information office**, explicitly recommended for touring the inner-bay area `[C-tr]`. Wikivoyage quotes the loop bus at **¥200 (¥100 child/disabled)** — **2018–2021 vintage, verify.**
- **Access in:** Tokyo → Tōhoku Shinkansen to **Ichinoseki** (~150 min, ~¥13,000) → **JR Ōfunato Line to Kesennuma** (~85 min, ¥1,230); or bus Ichinoseki–Kesennuma (~75 min, ¥1,090, 2–4/day). Sendai → Ichinoseki → Ōfunato Line ≈ 2 hr total, or ~3 hr by direct bus from Sendai Station. `[C]` (japan-guide, updated Feb 2025; JNTO) **`[VERIFY]` all fares.**

### Closing days — the actual pattern I could confirm `[C]`
- **Sunday is the risky day**, not Monday: **the fish market is closed Sundays** (japan-guide) and the **brewery shops close Sundays/holidays (Otokoyama) or are Mon–Fri only (Kakuboshi direct shop)**. So a Sunday-morning-arrival plan loses the market *and* both breweries.
- **Umi no Ichi:** irregular closures **Jan–June**, **no closures July–Dec**. Two Umi no Ichi shops (Hamaki, Kani Monogatari) close **Wednesdays**.
- **Mishione Yokochō staggers its closures deliberately** — Tue (Warung Mahal, Butayarō), Wed (TEN(T)), Thu (PRISM), Sun (Cheers) — so **something is always open**, a good line for copy.
- **Karakuwa Banya oyster hut: weekends only, and only in oyster season (~Oct–May).** **Yamayo Dining (Oshima): closed Tuesdays, lunch only 11:00–14:00 LO 13:30, 12 seats, reservations strongly advised.** **Karakuwa Visitor Center: closed Tuesdays.** Oshima's 青と緑の茶や haru: weekends only.
- **Many places are lunch-only or close early.** Confirmed lunch-only/early-close: Yamayo Dining (to 14:00), Tsurukame Shokudō (7:00–13:00), Banya (10:00–14:00), Hamaya (11:00–14:00), Uoken (to 16:00), Rias Kitchen dinner ends 20:00 **and 18:00 on weekdays**, Ichiba Sushi to 20:00 **but 19:00 on weekdays**. **Weekday evenings close notably earlier than weekends at Umi no Ichi.**
- **Seasonal menus genuinely disappear.** The sanma page states outright that the menus are "primarily offered in autumn 2025" and may end; hoya is **May–Aug only** at Yamayo Shokudō; meka-shabu is **Oct–Mar**; oysters **Oct–May**. Several restaurants explicitly ask you to **phone ahead to check the day's catch**. This is the single most important practical warning for a food-focused weekend.

### English menus and language — **NOT CONFIRMED. Say so.**
- I found **no source stating that English menus are common in Kesennuma.** Do not claim it.
- What IS confirmed: **the Kesennuma Tourism & Convention Bureau offers service in English** (JNTO). **Umi no Ichi 2F has a Tourist Service Center**; there is also a **Kesennuma Station-front tourist information office**. There is a full **English tourism site (en.kesennuma-kanko.jp)** and an English DMO site (visit-kesennuma.com).
- Counter-evidence pointing to *limited* English at venue level: the **market morning tour charges +¥6,000 for an English guide**; the **brewery tour offers English only for groups of 11+**; the **oyster raft tour charges +¥10,000 for an English guide**; **Karakuwa Banya explicitly says phone staff are Japanese-only, use email for other languages**; **the fish market's own website has essentially no English.** Fair inference for copy: *assume Japanese-language service and Japanese menus outside the DMO-facilitated experiences; picture menus and translation apps recommended.* Mark that as inference, not fact.

### Cash vs card — **NOT CONFIRMED. Say so.**
- **No official page I fetched states payment methods for any Kesennuma restaurant, shop, or market.** naiwan.info, uminoichi.com, kesennuma-mishione.com all omit it.
- The only relevant confirmed datum: **KESENNUMA Crewship (crewship.net)** is a local **app-based cashless points/loyalty card**, "the card that builds Kesennuma's future," with **~68,069 members and 100+ affiliated merchants** across restaurants, shops, hotels and services. It demonstrates meaningful local cashless infrastructure and is an excellent directory of local businesses by category — but it is a **local loyalty scheme, not a tourist payment solution**, and I could not confirm whether visitors can practically use it.
- **Safe copy:** "carry cash; small independent izakaya and market stalls in regional Japanese fishing ports frequently do not take cards" — flag as general advice, not Kesennuma-specific fact. **Do not state a cash-only or card-accepted policy for any named venue.**

### Other planning notes `[C]`
- **Book ahead where seats are few:** Yamayo Dining has **12 seats**; Banya caps at 30; Brewer's Table max 8; cooking class max 10. Experience booking deadlines are **3 days (market tour), 5 days (Brewer's Table), 7 days (brewery tour, cooking class), 2 weeks (oyster raft)** — a spontaneous weekend will miss most of them.
- **Food-relevant festivals `[C]`, `[VERIFY]` dates annually:** **Minato Matsuri 1–2 August**; **Bonito Festival (かつお祭) 7–11 October**; **Umi no Ichi Sanma Festival ~18 October**; **Meguro Sanma Festival in Tokyo ~12 October** (Kesennuma-supplied, off-site); **Uni-Hoya Festival** in June; **「今が旬！冬のメカジキ生食フェア」winter swordfish raw-eating fair, ~17 Nov–17 Dec** (a Miyagi Prefecture-run campaign); **Naiwan Morning Market (ないわん朝市)** — **first Saturday-ish of the month, 7:00–15:00 at 南町海岸公園 / Naiwan Plaza; a Night Market (夜市) 16:00–21:00 later in the month with bonito sashimi/tataki giveaways (100 servings at 16:30 and 17:30), local sake and craft beer.** Confirmed for July 2026: **morning market Sat 4 July, night market Sat 18 July** (page published 27 June 2026 — genuinely current). Organiser: Kesennuma Regional Development Co., 0226-48-5091. Also **「気仙沼みなとでマルシェ」** runs on scheduled dates.
- **Halal / dietary:** **Warung Mahal at Mishione Yokochō is halal-certified and there is a musholla (prayer room) in the alley** — a rare and genuinely useful fact for inbound copy. `[C]`
- **Beach season** opened 3 July in 2026 `[C]` (relevant to summer-weekend framing).
